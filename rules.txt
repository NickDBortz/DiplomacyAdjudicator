Basic Rules for Diplomacy:
1. There are a number of territories, including countries and oceans/lakes X
2. Each team starts with control of a subset of territories X
3. Each territory can be a supply center X
4. Each team owns troops equal to the number of primary territories they control
5. Each turn, every team can direct each of their troops to take a certain action X
6. Only one troop can occupy a territory at a time X
7. The first team to reach 18 points, which is primary territories, wins

Troop Types:
1. Army (land) X
2. Fleet (water/coast) X

Actions:
1. Hold (Do nothing) X
2. Transform (Convert an army to a fleet and vice versa if on a valid territory) X
3. Advance (move from a territory to a valid adjacent territory) X
4. Support (support another troop's action in a valid adjacent territory) X
5. Convoy (Utilize a fleet or fleets to transport an army over a body of water)

Milestone 1 (Army & Territory): COMPLETE
One team has one army on one territory with nowhere to go
Team 1 gets army from owning territory
Team 1 can use hold action

Milestone 2 (Single Advance): COMPLETE
One team has one army on one territory with one adjacent territory
Said army is able to advance onto the adjacent territory

Milestone 3 (Unobtrusive Advance): COMPLETE
Two teams, each with one territory and one army
Each team has a different adjacent territory that they advance into

Milestone 4 (Bounce): COMPLETE
Two teams, each with one territory and one army
Each team has a shared adjacent territory that they attempt to advance into and bounce back

Milestone 5 (Circular Advance): COMPLETE
Three teams, each with one territory and one army
Each team moves to advance in a circular motion into another team's territory and succeed

Milestone 6 (Supported Advance): COMPLETE
Three teams, each with one territory and one army
Two teams advance into a shared adjacent territory and team 3 supports team 2's advance, causing it to succeed

Milestone 7 (Supported Hold): COMPLETE
Three teams, each with one territory and one army
One team holds position, supported by team two. Team 3 advances on it, getting bounced back

Milestone 8 (Bounced Hold): COMPLETE
Three teams, each with one territory and one army
One team holds position. Team 2 advances on it, supported by team 3. Team 1 gets bounced to an adjacent empty territory

Milestone 9 (Cut Support): COMPLETE
Three teams, each with one territory and one army
Team 1 advances into Team 3, supported by Team 2. Team 3 advances on Team 2, cutting Support, bouncing all advances

Milestone 10 (Bounced Support): COMPLETE
Four teams, each with one territory and one army
Team 1 advances into Team 3, supported by Team 2. Team 3 advances on Team 2, supported by Team 4, taking Team 2 and boucing it to an adjacent empty territory

Milestone 11 (Transformations): COMPLETE
One team with three territories and one army
One territory is a land, one a coast, and one a water. Team 1 advances from land to coast, transforms, and advances from coast to water

Milestone 12 (Validation): COMPLETE
Invalid actions, like traversal to inaccessible locations, invalid transformations, etc. are removed automatically

Milestone 13 (Single Convoy): COMPLETE
One teams, with two territories, one army and one fleet
Team 1 has an army and convoys using it's Fleet to another land tile

Milestone 14 (Multiple Convoy): COMPLETE
One team with one army and three fleets
Team 1 has an army and convoys across three fleets to another land tile

Milestone 15 (Interrupted Convoy): COMPLETE
Team 1 has an army and attempts to convoy across three fleets to another land tile. The middle fleet gets attacked by an enemy fleet and breaks the chain

Milestone 16 (Convoy to Circular Advance): COMPLETE
Team 1 convoys an army into a tile that is occupied, but the occupant is advancing into the army's original tile

Milestone 17 (Convoy Blocked): COMPLETE
Team 1 convoys an army into a tile that is occupied and the occupant is not moving

Milestone 18 (Convoy Chain Halted): COMPLETE
Team 1 convoys an army into a tile that is occupied and the occupant is advancing into another tile. That advance fails, blocking the convoy

Milestone 19 (Dislodge Advancing Unit): COMPLETE
Team 1 advances on Team 2. Team 2 holds, blocking the advance and bouncing back. Team 3 advances on Team 1 with support, taking the spot and dislodging Team 1

Milestone 20 (Fixed Convoy): COMPLETE
Convoy is only an order fleet units can take. The army being convoyed will use an advance order to utilize the convoy

Milestone 21 (Order Writing): COMPLETE
Hold Order: TROOP_TYPE_PREFIX TERRITORY_ABBR H 
Advance Order: TROOP_TYPE_PREFIX TERRITORY_ABBR-TERRITORY_ABBR
Support Order: TROOP_TYPE_PREFIX TERRITORY_ABBR S ORDER
Transform Order: TROOP_TYPE_PREFIX TERRITORY_ABBR T
Convoy Order: TROOP_TYPE_PREFIX TERRITORY_ABBR C ADVANCE_ORDER
